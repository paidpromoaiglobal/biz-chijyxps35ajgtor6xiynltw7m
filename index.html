Our Story

Chinnis Beauty Salon

Chinnis Beauty Salon is Bhubaneswar's premier destination for beauty, wellness, and self-care, nestled in the heart of Sailashree Vihar near the iconic Jagannath Temple. With a passionate team of skilled stylists, therapists, and beauty experts, we bring you transformative experiences — from stunning hair highlights and precision cuts to deeply relaxing body massages and luxurious spa treatments. Our commitment to quality, attention to detail, and personalized care has earned us a remarkable 4.9-star rating from over 645 happy clients.

At Chinnis, we believe every visit should leave you feeling refreshed, confident, and radiant. Whether you're stepping in for a quick touch-up or a full beauty makeover, our courteous and professional staff ensure you receive nothing but the best. We also offer an exclusive clothing and accessories section, making us your one-stop destination for beauty and style. Open seven days a week from early morning to late evening, we are always here to make you look and feel your absolute best.
